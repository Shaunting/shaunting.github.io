Projects

Healthcare AI · Agents · Current research
Agentic Clinical Scoring for ER Triage
A multi-agent pipeline on two clinical datasets (MIMIC-IV-ED, UMN). AI agents propose clinical concepts and turn them into tick-box disease risk cards, with human-in-the-loop review at each step. Open-weight LLMs run on-premises under HIPAA, and every extraction is validated against human labels. Cuts expert review from weeks to 1 hour. Working toward publication.
AI · Production
uNote AI Tutor
An AI tutor that guides students to the answer instead of handing it over. RAG in FastAPI with pgvector, multi-LLM routing (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini), streaming, memory, and guardrails. Shipped with Django, Docker, and CI/CD for 16,000+ concurrent users.

AI · Health
AI Clinical Documentation System
A Flask pipeline that transcribes doctor-patient visit audio with the Whisper API, extracts structured notes with the Gemini API, and stores them in PostgreSQL. End to end, with no manual entry.
AI Research
LLM Adversarial Robustness in Educational AI
An LLM evaluation for AI safety. Attacked two open-weight models' guardrails with adversarial prompts in LangChain and HuggingFace, then measured system prompt compliance across 180 outputs with an LLM-as-judge and an evaluation rubric.
